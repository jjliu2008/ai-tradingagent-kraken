Volume

VWAP FIXED
session_vwap
cumsum(vwap_k · vol) / cumsum(vol)
Resets at UTC midnight. Uses vwap_k
(true TWAP per bar), not close·vol.
Fixed: was using close × volume.
distance_from_vwap
(close − session_vwap) / session_vwap
Filter: ≤ 0.03 (within 3% of VWAP).
close_location
(close − low) / (high − low)
0=bar low, 1=bar high.
Core signal quality gate.
Bar Structure NEW
body_ratio
|close − open| / (high − low)
0 = doji, 1 = full-body candle.
Filters out indecision bars.
upper_wick / lower_wick
upper = (high − max(O,C)) / range
lower = (min(O,C) − low) / range
Fraction of bar range above/below body.
wick_imbalance
lower_wick − upper_wick
>0 = buying pressure (L2 proxy).
>0.10 = clear buyer dominance.
micro_pressure
(close − bar_mid) / range
bar_mid = (high+low)/2
+0.5 = close at bar top.
Proxy for bid-side strength.
trade_density_ratio
count / SMA(count, 20).shift(1)
High = many small trades (retail).
Low = fewer large trades (institutional).

PnL Math
Round-trip cost verification
entry_price = open_next_bar × (1 + 0.0026 + 0.0005) target_price = entry_price × (1 + target_pct) exit_price = target_price × (1 − 0.0031) pnl = (exit − entry) / entry − 0.0031 → runner TAKE_PROFIT: +4.863% (target 5.5%, cost ~0.637%) → runner STOP_LOSS: −2.115% (stop 1.5%, cost ~0.615%)
Commission charged effectively 1.5× round-trip — slightly conservative vs actual Kraken costs (0.62% actual vs 0.62% model). Verified against observed trade PnLs.
